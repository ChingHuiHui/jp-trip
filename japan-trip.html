Hi teacher ♡

I wanted to share my recent trip to Japan with you!

This one was a little different — I started off traveling alone, then met up with friends, went to a concert, Disney, and even a lake town. Every day had something new.

I put it all together as a little photo diary, so you can see what it was like. Hope you enjoy reading it ☺

with love,
me ♡

01
✈️Flying solo to Japan
from airport to hotel ~

I really don't love flying

To be honest, I'm not a fan of flying at all. Take-off and landing always make me so nervous. ><

So I prepared a bunch of movies in advance and watched them the whole flight to distract myself from thinking about the plane shaking.

the moment I landed: I did it !!
solo trip in-flight 🎬 nervous me
page 3
02
🌸Wandering around
just walking, no plan ~

A perfect day for a walk

The weather on my first day was surprisingly lovely — warm sunshine and a gentle breeze. I didn't have any plans, so I just walked around and popped into any shop that caught my eye.

Sometimes the best part of traveling is having no plan at all ☺

page 4
03
👯Meeting up with friends
slowly, one by one ~

Everyone arrived separately

My friends and I didn't fly out together — instead, we arrived one by one.

Some came earlier, some came later. At airports, train stations, hotel lobbies, we kept saying "Oh, you're here too!" to each other.

As we grow up, traveling with friends gets harder — everyone has their own schedule. So when we do manage to gather, it feels extra precious ♡

this is what grown-up travel looks like ~
page 5
04
💖TWICE concert!
the main event ✦

The reason I came to Japan ♡

At Japan National Stadium — the 80,000-seat Tokyo Olympics venue — TWICE became the first K-pop group ever to perform there! With a 360° stage and moving carts, fans on every side got to see them up close.

The "One Spark" fireworks were so loud they actually paused the baseball game next door 😂 10+ year fan here — politics kept them out of Taiwan for ages, so every flight to see them was worth it ♡

National Stadium first K-pop One Spark 🎆
page 6
05
🏰Tokyo Disney
rainy magical day ☂

Pouring before it even started

It started pouring rain first thing in the morning ☔ So we made an emergency stop at a convenience store to grab some rain ponchos.

Soaked from the start, but our excitement wasn't dampened one bit — we walked into the park ready for magic anyway ✨

a bit of a mess before it even began ♡
Tokyo Disney pouring rain ☔ rain poncho
page 7
05
🎠Tokyo Disney, cont.
rides, shows & characters ~

So many rides... and characters ♡

Despite the rain, we still braved Journey to the Center of the Earth, Raging Spirits, Rapunzel's Lantern Festival, Tower of Terror... and caught the "Dance the Globe!" show with characters dancing from all over.

The best part? Meeting so many characters in person — Mickey, Donald, Goofy, Jasmine, Aladdin... I'd never had that experience at Disney before, and it became my favorite memory at park ♡

soaked, but smiling all day ♡
page 8
06
🏔️Lake Kawaguchi
our first day by the lake ~

Day one by the lake

We rented e-scooters to roam around the little town and take in the scenery, then booked a private tennis court — and somehow you could see Mt. Fuji right from the court! We were sure the clouds would block it... but there it was 🗻

For dinner we hit the supermarket together, brought everything back, and cooked sukiyaki Japanese hot pot with thinly sliced beef dipped in raw egg as a group ♡

page 9
06
🗻Mt. Fuji moment
the sky cleared up ~

Roller coaster + a Fuji surprise

The next two days were pretty rainy, but we still made it out to a few spots. The highlight was hitting an amusement park and riding one of its four roller coasters — so tall, so long, totally worth queuing in the wet for!

On our last day leaving the town, the sky finally cleared up — and there it was, Mt. Fuji in full view 🗻 Left feeling like nothing was missing.

amusement park roller coasters 🎢 Mt. Fuji
page 10
07
🍻Food & drinks
yakiniku nights ~

Nights with friends

You can't go to Japan and not eat yakiniku Japanese-style grilled beef you cook yourself at the table!

Add a drink, and we're suddenly talking about everything — the day, old memories, future plans.

cheers! to this trip ♡

🎫Heading home
staff ticket flight ~

Flying back on a staff ticket

The way home was a little special — I flew back on a staff ticket a special discounted ticket through an airline connection. The check-in was different and I was a bit anxious, but I felt really grateful when I finally got on the plane.
